# Muisnow Devs Privacy Policy

> This version of the Privacy Policy uses translation tools from the original Chinese version. In case of any discrepancy, the original Chinese version shall prevail.

Welcome to Muisnow Devs. We value your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information transparently and responsibly. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information. It applies to:

* Users of the Muisnow Devs websites, applications, products, or services
* Users who sign in or authenticate with MuID
* Users who upload, submit, or provide content to Muisnow Devs

## I. Definitions

Within this policy:

* **"Muisnow Devs"** refers to all websites, applications, backend services, and other services provided by Muisnow Devs.
* **"We", "us", "the service"** refer to Muisnow Devs.
* **"User", "you"** refer to any individual who uses the service, signs in with MuID, or provides content or data to the service.
* **"User-generated content (UGC)"** means any content you upload, submit, or store in the service, such as text, images, configuration files, usage records, and other data not automatically produced by the system.

## II. Data Collection

We collect data only to the extent required to deliver the service. This includes information you voluntarily provide and information generated automatically when the service operates.

### A. Information You Provide

If you register an account, use MuID, or access features that require uploads, we may collect:

* Username
* Email address
* MuID account information
* User-generated content (UGC)
* Feedback, suggestions, or contact details you choose to submit
* Application error logs (only when you opt in to upload them)

### B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the service, we may automatically collect:

* IP address
* Device information (such as device model and operating system version)
* Usage records and timestamps
* Application version and interaction events

## III. Use of User-Generated Content (UGC)

If you upload or submit content to the service, you acknowledge and agree that:

1. You retain ownership of your content.
2. You grant Muisnow Devs permission to use the content for delivering the service, maintaining systems, improving features, and enhancing security.
3. Your content may, **after being anonymised and irreversibly de-identified**, be used to:
   * Improve product functionality
   * Train models or analyse service quality
   * Assist with debugging and enhance the overall experience
4. We will not disclose, sell, or share personally identifiable content unless it complies with legal requirements or you give explicit consent.

If you delete your content, we will remove or anonymise it within a reasonable period. However, we may retain certain information as required by law.

## IV. Generative AI and Third-Party Services

### A. Use of Third-Party Generative AI

The service may rely on third-party generative AI technologies (for example, for recognition, content generation, or error analysis). When you use these features:

* Your data, including UGC, may be transmitted to third-party services for processing as needed.
* Third-party services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

**You must agree to the relevant third-party privacy terms before using AI-powered features.**

We minimise, anonymise, and limit the data we share, and we only transmit the content required to provide the feature.

### B. Third-Party Policies

If a feature relies on a third-party service, you are also subject to that third party's privacy policy. If you do not agree with those terms, please do not use the feature.

## V. Use of Data

We use your information only for:

* Providing, maintaining, and improving the service
* Authenticating your identity (including MuID sign-in flows)
* Troubleshooting issues and enhancing stability and security
* Responding to your inquiries, feedback, or issues
* Sending notifications and important updates
* Complying with legal obligations or regulatory requests

We do not sell or rent your personal information for advertising purposes.

## VI. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties unless one of the following applies:

* You have given explicit consent
* Sharing is necessary for service providers who help us deliver the service (and who are bound by confidentiality obligations)
* Compliance is required by law or court order
* We detect security, fraud, or abuse issues that must be investigated

## VII. Information Security

We implement reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your data, including:

* Encrypted transmission
* Access controls
* Risk monitoring and anomaly detection
* Least-privilege practices

However, no internet transmission is perfectly secure. We continuously work to reduce risk, but we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## VIII. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

* Access and obtain copies of your data
* Correct or update information
* Delete data
* Withdraw consent (which may affect certain services)

To exercise these rights, contact us and we will handle your request within a reasonable timeframe.

## IX. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page, and significant changes may be communicated through additional notices. Please review this page regularly for the latest information.
