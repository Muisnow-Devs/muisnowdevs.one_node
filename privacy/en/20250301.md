# Muisnow Devs Privacy Policy (March 1, 2025 Edition)

> This version of the Privacy Policy uses translation tools from the original Chinese version. In case of any discrepancy, the original Chinese version shall prevail.

Thank you for using the services provided by Muisnow Devs. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide.

## I. Definitions

The terms used in this privacy policy are defined as follows:

* "Muisnow Devs" refers to the Muisnow Devs website and its services.
* "We" and "our" refer to Muisnow Devs.
* "You" and "your" refer to users who visit or register for an account with Muisnow Devs.

## II. Data Collection

We do not actively collect your personal information unless you choose to provide it. You may choose to share the following details:

* Username
* Email address
* Account information
* Application error logs

When you communicate with our servers, we automatically collect the following information:

* Your IP address
* Device identifiers
* Access time

## III. Use of Data

We use the information you provide only in the following situations:

* Delivering and improving our services
* Responding to your inquiries or requests
* Processing error reports to enhance your experience
* Complying with legal obligations

## IV. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent. Any sharing of information will follow your authorization, except in the following circumstances:

* Compliance with laws and regulations
* Requests from judicial authorities

## V. Information Security

We take appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will make every effort to safeguard your data, while acknowledging that absolute security cannot be ensured.

## VI. User Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.

## VII. Changes to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this privacy policy. Any changes will be announced on this page. Please check this page regularly to stay informed of the latest updates.

## VIII. Contact Us
